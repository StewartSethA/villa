Is the yellow-ringed cell on a papyrus sheet?
How to review (read once)
  • Each sample is one surface cell that a growth run produced. Decide from the CT whether that cell lies on a papyrus sheet, is not on a sheet (in the gap between sheets, in air, or cutting across sheets), or you are unsure.
  • The two sections contain the surface normal (vertical, +normal up) and one surface direction (horizontal). A cell on a sheet sits on a bright band that runs horizontally through the ring. Cyan dots are the surface itself near that section.
  • The three planes are parallel to the surface, 4 voxels below, at, and above it. The xy view is the ordinary CT slice at the cell's z, with the surface trace in cyan.
  • You are blind to whether the guard removed or kept the cell. The surface-prediction overlay (the input the check under review uses) is hidden. Showing it is allowed, and it is recorded per sample.
  • Keys: 1 sheet · 2 not sheet · 3 unsure · p toggle prediction · ←/→ previous/next. Progress is saved in this browser; export when done.
